# 1. The narrative hides

## The 15% is half the story

When the Italian *regime forfettario* comes up, the number you hear first
is **15%**: the substitute IRPEF rate that a self-employed worker under the
forfettario regime pays instead of the ordinary progressive scale
(23-43%).

Fifteen percent. Sounds high, sounds low. Depends on where you look.

## Where supporters look

Supporters say: *"It's a 60% cut compared to ordinary IRPEF. For low
earners, it's a lifeline."*

True, in part. But the number is incomplete.

## Where critics look

Critics say: *"It costs the State €3.4 billion per year. It distorts
competition with employees. It creates structural tax evasion."*

Also true, in part. But the number is also incomplete.

## What's missing in both narratives

Neither narrative looks at the **real net in pocket at the end of the
month**. Let's try.

### On €100 received, the forfettario keeps:

| Item | Amount | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Gross receipt | €100.00 | Invoice issued |
| Stamp duty | −€2.00 | Above €77.59 (L. 71/2013) |
| Forfettizzato income (avg coeff. 78%) | base | Coefficient for ATECO type "consulting" |
| Substitute IRPEF 15% on forfettizzato income | −€11.70 | Example: 78% × €100 × 15% |
| INPS Separate Management 26.07% | −€20.34 | On forfettizzato income (78% × €100) |
| Accountant | −€3.00 | Conservative annual estimate divided |
| Real management costs (software, connectivity, tools) | −€3.00 | Conservative estimate |
| **Net in pocket** | **≈ €60.00** | From which to cover living expenses |

And here the **real operating margin** — the difference between what
comes in and what goes out for life — is further eroded by rent, food,
health, transport, children. Real operators report an operating margin
often **at 10% of gross**.

> *"A forfettario without discounts on €100 received keeps 70/75 euro in
> pocket, from which they must also cover expenses. The narrative of 15%
> is misleading."*
> — MVB, 2026-06-29

## The 15% is real, but it's only one layer

The substitute 15% applies **only to forfettizzato income** (calculated
with a profitability coefficient per ATECO code, typically 67-86% of
turnover). It does not apply to:

- INPS Separate Management (≈ 26.07% for those without other funds)
- Regional and municipal surcharges (also for forfettari, in some cases)
- VAT (which the forfettario does not charge but pays as a consumer)
- Stamp duty
- Waste, services taxes, etc.

The 15% is a **skylight** on a larger structure. Looking at it alone is
like looking at a skylight and thinking it's the ceiling.

## What we need to see the truth

Three numbers side by side, always:

1. **Nominal substitute rate**: 15% (5% first 5 years if requirements met)
2. **INPS Separate Management contribution cost**: 26.07% (subjects
   without other funds)
3. **Real living costs**: accountant, software, mandatory training,
   insurance, real management expenses

With these three numbers we can talk about the "effective rate" without
being fooled by narrative.
