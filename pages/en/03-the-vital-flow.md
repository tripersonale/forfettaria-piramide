# 3. The vital flow

## The worker's life cycle

A person, in their working life, naturally traverses the three faces in
an order that reflects their energy, responsibilities, and risk appetite.

```
Young / growing              Mature / in vision              Senior / in peace
       │                              │                            │
       ▼                              ▼                            ▼
   FORFETTARIO                   ENTREPRENEUR                 EMPLOYEE
   (experiment)                  (create and hire)            (operate with care)
```

## Phase 1 — Forfettario when young

When young, you have:

- Little market experience (you need to learn)
- Little own capital (you can't risk everything)
- High risk tolerance but low capacity to absorb it
- Tendency to want to **experiment** before committing

The forfettario is perfect: zero capital, you learn the market directly,
you grow by trial. If you fail, you fail alone. No one fired, no
creditors.

## Phase 2 — Entrepreneur (optional)

If the vision holds, if the market responds, if own capital has
accumulated: you can **jump** to the enterprise form. SRL or partnership.
You start hiring, structuring, scaling.

This jump is not mandatory. Many stay forfettari for life, by choice. It
is a legitimate choice.

## Phase 3 — Employee when senior

With age, weights change:

- Less energy to risk
- More need for stability
- Full contributions give a higher pension
- Desire to dedicate oneself to the craft, not to bureaucracy

Becoming an employee again — or **becoming an employee by choice** — is
a natural path. The mature worker brings experience and vision into
someone else's organization, and the organization welcomes them as a
resource.

## The "cliff" effect is the real problem

Today the system **breaks the flow** with a wall: at €85,000 turnover you
jump from 15% to 23-43% + VAT + ordinary bookkeeping. It's a leap that
pushes you to **not grow**, to stay small to keep the regime.

> *"To contrast the growth problem, just make it easier to use forfettari
> within SRLs."*
> — MVB, 2026-06-27

The solution is not to abolish the forfettario. It is to **remove the
cliff** and allow a gradual transition that keeps the vital flow open.

## The "cap limit" as a milestone

A concrete proposal: instead of the wall at €85,000 turnover, a **cap on
real net income** (e.g. €5,000-€10,000 net per month). Whoever reaches
it stably is **invited** (not forced) to make the quality jump to the
SRL — a collective category contractor.

The cap becomes a milestone, not a wall.
