# 2. The three faces of work

## The model

Work in a complex society has **three legitimate forms**, each with its
own dignity, risk and social function.

| Face | Form | Risk | Social function |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Employee** | Service in someone else's enterprise | Low (salary ceiling, holidays, TFR, welfare) | Operate — execute with care what others have thought |
| **The Forfettario** | Atomic collaborator, self-employed | Medium (income cap, no welfare, heavy INPS) | Explore — test one's capacity in the market |
| **The Entrepreneur** | Creator of matter from vision | High (own capital, unlimited liability, exposed market) | Create — take the risk of generating value others do not yet see |

## The employee

> *"The employee pays more but sleeps peacefully. They are an operator."*

Pays more in marginal rate (full IRPEF + surcharges + employee
contributions). In exchange they get:

- Fixed monthly salary
- Paid holidays, thirteenth month, severance (TFR)
- Social safety nets (NASpI, redundancy fund)
- Full health and pension coverage
- Someone else decides what to do, when, how

It is not an inferior condition. It is a life choice. Many choose it
because they want to **specialize in one thing and do it well**, without
having to think about the client, the invoice, the bookkeeping.

## The forfettario

> *"A small entrepreneur of oneself, agent of good, with a cap limit."*

Pays less in substitute rate but bears more in welfare and management
costs. In exchange they get:

- Total flexibility on clients, hours, rates
- Minimum own capital to start
- Limited liability (not unlimited like the entrepreneur)
- Direct market learning: what is worth, how much, for whom
- Possibility to experiment without firing anyone

It is the **natural phase of those who are growing**: young
professionals, digital artisans, consultants, technicians who want to
measure themselves in the market without launching a full enterprise.

## The entrepreneur

> *"The entrepreneur risks and puts their soul into creating matter from
> vision."*

Pays ordinary IRPEF plus IRES (on the SRL), plus contributions, plus VAT.
In exchange they get:

- Capacity to hire, organize, scale
- Access to credit (when available)
- Full responsibility for the vision and for the workers
- Possibility to transform an idea into employment for others

It is the phase of those who have found a vision that holds and decide
to take the risk of bringing it to scale.

## Why they are complementary, not in competition

A healthy society has neither only employees (rigid hierarchy) nor only
entrepreneurs (market anarchy). It has a **dynamic flow** between the
three forms, reflecting life phase, risk appetite, vision maturity.

The forfettario **is the bridge** between employee (security) and
entrepreneur (full risk). Without forfettari, the leap is too big: you
either stay an employee for life, or you dive straight into the
enterprise.

## The European mistake

When the EU looks at the forfettario it sees only an "Italian fiscal
anomaly" to correct. It does not see that it is **social
infrastructure**: it produces the chain that takes competence to market
and then to enterprise.

Removing the forfettario means removing the bridge. You do not eliminate
the anomaly: you eliminate the transition.
