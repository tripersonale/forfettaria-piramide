# 6. Sources and data

All data cited in this document has an institutional or authoritative
third-party source, linked below.

## Official Italian sources

- **Agenzia delle Entrate** — IRPEF rates and forfettario regime
  https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/web/guest/imposte/regime-forfettario
- **INPS** — Gestione Separata, rates for self-employed
  https://www.inps.it/it/it/inps-comunica/dati-e-bilanci/gestioni-e-bilanci/gestione-separata.html
- **MEF** — Report on the unobserved economy and tax/social contribution
  evasion (historical series tax gap)
  https://www.mef.gov.it/focus/Relazione-sull-economia-non-osservata-e-sull-evasione-fiscale-e-contributiva
- **Corte dei Conti** — Report on the forfettario regime (24 June 2026)
  https://www.corteconti.it/

## Official EU sources

- **European Commission** — Country Report Italy 2026 (3 June 2026)
  https://economy-finance.ec.europa.eu/publications_en
- **EU VAT Directive** — small business exemption thresholds
  https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32006L0112
