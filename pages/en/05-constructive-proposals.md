# 5. Constructive proposals

## Premise

These proposals are not "saving the forfettario". They are **tools to
make the Italian fiscal system coherent with the digital age, AI, and
European free movement**, keeping the forfettario as start-up
infrastructure.

## 1. Cap limit on real net (instead of turnover threshold)

**Today**: wall at €85,000 annual turnover → jump to 23-43% + VAT.

**Proposal**: cap on **monthly real net income** of the forfettario.
Whoever stably keeps more than €5,000-€10,000 net per month is
**invited** (not forced) to make the jump to the SRL.

**Effect**: the cap becomes a milestone, not punishment. Whoever wants
to stay small stays. Whoever grows sees the limit as a milestone, not as
a wall.

## 2. Collective category SRL contractor

**Today**: the forfettario who wants to grow has to set up an SRL alone,
with notary, accounting, management costs of €5-10k/year minimum.

**Proposal**: the **category SRL** — a collective contractor in which
multiple forfettari enter as partners, share governance, accounting,
infrastructure, and can grow without losing autonomy.

**Effect**: the jump from forfettario to enterprise becomes a **natural
evolution** inside a familiar ecosystem, not a traumatic individual
operation.

## 3. Dynamic balancing employees-collaborators

**Today**: every enterprise has few tools to choose the optimal mix
between employees and forfettario collaborators.

**Proposal**: enterprises that maintain an **equilibrated mix** between
employees (for stable functions) and forfettario collaborators (for
variable and proximity functions) get access to **tax credits** on
training and innovation.

**Effect**: the system rewards whoever maintains social fabric (stable
employees) **and** whoever uses the forfettario as proximity accelerator.
Neither becomes a substitute for the other.

## 4. Voluntary transparency as a shield

**Today**: the forfettario is suspected of evasion by default.

**Proposal**: whoever joins **voluntarily** a digital transparency
protocol (complete electronic invoicing, banking tracking, Open VAT
Number with public dashboard) gets **decennial stability of the regime**
and access to a higher turnover threshold (e.g. €120,000).

**Effect**: whoever is honest is rewarded with stability and growth
space. Whoever evades stays exposed to the current regime. *Armor, not
vault.*

## 5. Constitutionality: art. 35, 41, 53

The forfettario regime does not violate the Italian Constitution, it
realises it:

- **Art. 35**: *"The Republic protects work in all its forms."* The
  forfettario is a form of work, not an exception.
- **Art. 41**: *"Private economic initiative is free."* The forfettario
  is the legal tool to exercise it without patrimonial barriers.
- **Art. 53**: *"Everyone is required to contribute to public spending
  according to their contributory capacity."* Progressivity applies to
  the **system** (life cycle: forfettario → enterprise → employee), not
  to the single year.

## 6. The age of AI agents: forfettario as outpost

AI agents (claude-code, codestral, hermes-agent, gemini-cli, qwen-code,
kimi-code, mavis-agent and others) are today individual tools, not
enterprise tools. A single forfettario with a well-configured AI agent
can serve 5-10 clients with enterprise quality.

**Removing the forfettario = removing from Europe 3.8 million
AI-amplified territorial outposts**, to the advantage of 5 global
platforms that have no local rooting.
