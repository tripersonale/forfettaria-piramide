## 30-second summary

- **The 15% is misleading.** On €100 received, the forfettario's real net
  in pocket is between €70 and €75, after substitute IRPEF, INPS Separate
  Management, stamp duty, accountant and real expenses. Operating margin
  is often around 10%.
- **The tax gap concerns all self-employed**, not only forfettari.
  Aggregating without disaggregating is a methodological error that leads
  to the wrong target.
- **Italy is below the EU average** for VAT exemption threshold (€85,000 vs
  €100,000 in France and Germany, €700,000 in Austria).
- **The three faces of work** — employee, forfettario, entrepreneur — are
  three legitimate and complementary forms. The forfettario is not a
  privilege: it is lightweight start-up infrastructure, a natural phase of
  the working life cycle.
- **In the age of AI agents**, removing the forfettario means handing the
  market over to 5 global players. A Europe that wants digital sovereignty
  cannot simultaneously destroy the only vehicle that produces atomic
  entrepreneurs on the ground.
